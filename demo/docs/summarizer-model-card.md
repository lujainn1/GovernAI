# Model card — Internal Meeting Notes Summarizer

## Purpose
Summarizes internal meeting transcripts for staff who could not attend. The
summary is a draft: the meeting owner reviews and edits every summary before it
is shared with anyone else.

## Data
Only transcripts classified public or internal are used. No personal, health,
financial or biometric data is processed; participant names are stripped from
the transcript before it leaves the wiki. Transcripts and summaries are deleted
after 90 days on a documented retention schedule, and the lawful basis for
processing is recorded in the RoPA.

## Vendor
Summaries are generated by a documented third-party LLM vendor under an
approved data processing agreement, reviewed by legal on 2025-11-04. The vendor
does not train on submitted content and retains no request logs.

## Security and access
Encryption in transit and at rest. Access is read-only and least-privilege:
only the meeting's participants can request a summary of that meeting.

## Transparency and oversight
Every summary is labelled "AI-generated draft — review before sharing", so
users always know they are reading model output. Every request is written to an
audit log retained for one year. A human owner approves each summary; the
system cannot post, email or publish anything by itself.
